New since the last build (short version: TestFlight allows 4,000 characters)

Smart Planner
- No more invented dinners: meals/breaks only when notes ask.
- Plan a future day (Day picker, 60 days out); Save Plan → My Day → Coming Up.
- Change the Plan: walking + standby time shown ("~X min standing in line"); one-tap Less Walking/Must-Dos First/Shortest Waits report if either changed; Apple Intelligence text requests.
- Rain AND heat plan: indoor rides move into rainy/hottest hours, heads-up + notification.

Rope Drop Plan: My Day → new sunrise icon — times your Must-Dos from today's actual park opening, ordered by walking distance (waits are near zero right at rope drop).

Wait Times: new ride cards; drag/tap panel header to expand/shrink; my-location button; pull-to-refresh replaced by a tappable pill; shows open a show page with reminders.

Ride page: new layout; Wait Forecast replaces Predictions; "you usually wait ~X"; forecasts use everyone's recorded waits.

Single Rider: a busy ride (40+ min) suggests its Single Rider line (Universal only) or a similar shorter ride, tap to jump there. Also a small badge on ride cards/ride page/My Day picker regardless of wait, plus a "Single Rider Only" filter at Universal resorts.

Add to My Day: one screen — pick rides/a show, then Anytime/Now/Best time/showtime/set time.

Live Activities: resort colors, big countdowns, progress bars, 3-stop Next Up timeline, Done/Skip/"I'm On"/"Used It" buttons.

Alerts & stats: Must-Do down alerts; Crowd Calendar; Day recap (My Day → ✨), shareable.

Car locator: sections/rows match Disney/Universal apps (scroll wheel); auto-guesses from GPS.

Ride photos: Rode It! has an optional photo step; Visit History shows a thumbnail.

Trip budget + pacing: Spending → "Set a Trip Budget" — a progress bar, and "~$X/day for N more days" with a Trip set.

Offline, no misleading waits: with no signal, rides show "No signal" instead of a fake wait or false "Closed".

Lightning Lane: a paid Single Pass return logs its price into Spending automatically. Tip Board shows a "Worth it today?" card totaling minutes saved + real paid cost.

Character meet tracker: Bucket List's new "Characters" tab, 22 Orlando characters.

Bucket List restaurant refresh: Wolfgang Puck Bar & Grill and Thunder Falls Terrace permanently closed — CLOSED banner, rating kept. Hot Dog Hall of Fame® closed; Fat One's Hot Dogs & Italian Ice added as a separate new entry. Check: confirm any rating on these three is still there.

Data export: Settings → Data & Sync — CSV export for Bucket List restaurants/hotels/characters. Good backup before an update.

Trip vs. last visit: Visit History shows "This Trip So Far" vs. the same day-count into your previous trip (needs 2+ trips logged).

Tip Calculator: Settings → Tools — quick 15/18/20/25% or custom, split across your party.
