New since the last build (short version: TestFlight allows 4,000 characters)

Smart Planner
- No more invented dinners: meals/breaks only when your notes ask ("snack break at 3").
- Plan a future day (Day picker, 60 days out). Save Plan → My Day → Coming Up; the morning of, it becomes your live Next Up plan.
- Change the Plan: shows total walking; one-tap Less Walking/Must-Dos First/Shortest Waits; with Apple Intelligence, type requests.
- Rain AND heat plan: indoor rides move into likely-rain or hottest hours, with a heads-up note and a notification ~30 min before (Settings → Rain/Heat Heads-Up).

Wait Times: new ride cards (colored wait tile, Must-Do star); drag/tap the panel header to expand/shrink; my-location button; pull-to-refresh replaced by a tappable "N min ago" pill; shows open a show page with reminders.

Ride page: new layout (wait tile, 4 buttons); Wait Forecast replaces Predictions ("Go now"/"Shorter at 8pm", hourly bars); "you usually wait ~X" after timed rides; forecasts now use everyone's recorded waits (day one, not just your own history).

Add to My Day: one screen — pick several rides/a show, then Anytime/Now/Best time/showtime/set time.

Live Activities: resort colors, big countdowns, progress bars, a 3-stop Next Up timeline, and Done/Skip/"I'm On"/"Used It" buttons.

Alerts & stats: Must-Do down alerts; Crowd Calendar uses measured waits + predicts 2 weeks out; Day recap (My Day → ✨) with rides/steps/spending, shareable.

Car locator: sections/rows match the Disney/Universal apps (scroll wheel); opening it guesses your section and row from GPS automatically. Lot maps: Magic Kingdom, Animal Kingdom, EPCOT, Hollywood Studios, Epic Universe. Unmapped lots learn from saved spots. Check: section/row should fill in ("Guessed from…"); tell me if one's wrong.

Fixed: the upload rejected for Siri's "Plan my day" description mentioning Apple (App Store rule 90626).

Ride photos: Rode It! has an optional photo step; Visit History shows a thumbnail.

Trip budget: Spending → "Set a Trip Budget" — a progress bar tracks spend against it.

Offline, no more misleading wait times: with no signal, rides show "No signal" instead of a fake wait or false "Closed".

Lightning Lane cost auto-log: booking a paid Single Pass logs its return AND price into Spending automatically.

Character meet tracker: Bucket List's new "Characters" tab — a checklist (met/date/notes/photo), 22 Orlando characters seeded.

Bucket List restaurant refresh: fixed a few closed/changed restaurants.
- Wolfgang Puck Bar & Grill (Disney Springs) and Thunder Falls Terrace (Islands of Adventure) permanently closed — they now show a red CLOSED banner instead of vanishing, so any rating you gave them is kept.
- Hot Dog Hall of Fame® (CityWalk) closed; Fat One's Hot Dogs & Italian Ice (Joey Fatone's spot, same location) is added as a new entry — it's a different restaurant, not a rename, so the old one keeps its own rating and CLOSED banner.
- Check: if you'd rated any of these three, open Bucket List → Restaurants and confirm your rating is still there.
