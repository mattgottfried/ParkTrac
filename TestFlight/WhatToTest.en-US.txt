NEW: Car Locator

Save where you parked
• Tap the car button on the Wait Times map (top right) or "Save Parking Spot" in My Day → Parking.
• Standing by the car, tap "Save Spot Here" — ThrillTrack takes a precise GPS fix (a few seconds).
• Add the lot/row (e.g. "Zurg 112") and snap a photo of the row sign.

Find your car
• A blue car pin shows on the Wait Times map; the car button turns blue.
• Open it for a mini map, distance and walk time, and "Walk There in Maps" for Apple Maps walking directions.
• The spot and note sync to your other devices on the same iCloud account; the photo stays on the phone that took it.
• A spot lasts the park day (20 hours), then clears itself. "Move Spot to Here" and "Clear Spot" are in the sheet.
• No GPS (location off, deep garage)? The note and photo still save.

Please check:
• Save a spot outside, walk away, reopen the sheet — the distance and walk time look right, and Walk There opens Maps.
• Camera asks permission once, and the photo shows in the sheet (tap to enlarge).
• The car pin appears on the map, and tapping it opens the sheet.
• Instant Alerts from the last build still work.
