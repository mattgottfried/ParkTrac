Fixed since the last build

Smart Planner no longer adds a dinner you didn't pick
- Apple Intelligence was sometimes inventing a "Dinner at Be Our Guest" (even twice), which pushed your rides past park close.
- Now it only adds a meal or break when your notes ask for one (e.g. "dinner at 6:30"), and never the same one twice.
- Check: pick a few rides with no notes → Plan with Apple Intelligence → only your rides and chosen shows appear, no extra dining.
- Check: add "snack break at 3" in the notes → one snack break at 3.
