New since the last build (short version: TestFlight allows 4,000 characters)

Pass Savings: new "I Renewed" button — snapshots your old pass into history and starts a new pass year; shows both "This Pass Year" and "Lifetime" savings. Once you've renewed at least once, also shows "Pass cost trend" ($X → $Y over N renewals) and "Best pass year" (your highest-savings year yet).

My Day: live "stood in line ~Xm today" line, now paired with a live "walked ~X mi today" line (re-polls the pedometer every minute). Visit History: "This Trip So Far" card adds "Best day so far" (most rides) and, when it's actually true, "Quietest trip yet" vs. every past trip's average crowd level — plus the existing spend-per-day comparison, "Most rides at [park]", and "N years running" streak.

Must-Do alerts: "back up" notifications say how long it was down and flag a short reset. Purple "One Last Ride?" capsule ~30 min before close. My Day: a dismissible "take a break" nudge after 4 back-to-back rides with no gap.

Challenges: 4 new badges (Early Bird, Night Owl, Short Wait Streak, Beat the Wait). Park Bingo also works zoomed out as "Resort Bingo", with by-ride-type and by-thrill-level breakdowns.

Wait Times: "Down Right Now" and "Nearby & Short" strips; wait tile trend arrows; "closing soon" banner + "Quietest/busiest" line; rest-stop button; ride page Loud/Dark/Sudden-moments chips; Easy Wins strip; "+15/-10 vs usual". Shows: a "Next after: …" hint when the next showing is too soon to reach.

Smart Planner & My Day: both flag free gaps ≥20 min and suggest a nearby short-wait ride; "On this day" card; Best Park Today; no invented dinners unless asked; plan a future day; Change the Plan shows standby time + walking with one-tap chips and Apple Intelligence requests; rain/heat routes indoor rides into bad weather.

Trip Planner: "Vacation Calendar" with a "Blocked Out" flag. Trip Budget pacing: "~$X/day for N more days".

Spending: "This Year" total; "Quick Snack" chips. My Dining: Mobile Order countdown.
