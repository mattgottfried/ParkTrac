New since the last build (short version: TestFlight allows 4,000 characters)

Fixed: Universal Orlando screens were saying "Lightning Lane" (Disney's branding) instead of "Express Pass" — the ride page chip, Add to My Day, My Day's section title, Tip Board, and the Spending category are now resort-correct. Also: plain Express Pass has no return time to book — the return-time card, ride card capsule/bell, Tip Board value, and "ll" Add to My Day option now only show at Universal when Express Now is on (Settings → Annual Passes). Marking a DAS/AAP return "used" (My Day checkbox or the Live Activity "Used It" button) now also logs the ride as ridden.

Challenges: 7 new badges — Rope Dropper, Deal Hunter, Marathoner, Coaster Collector, World Traveler, Park Completionist, Penny Pincher. Plus live callouts: "New personal best" ride day, "Already beat your last trip's total".

Pass Savings: "I Renewed" starts a new pass year, keeping "This Pass Year" and "Lifetime" separate. With renewal history, also shows "Pass cost trend", "Best pass year", and "Cost per visit" year over year. "Should I Upgrade?" compares your spend to the next tier's discount rate.

Ride milestones: a notification when a ride's count (or resort total) crosses 10/25/50/100/200/500/1000. Ride page shows "first ridden [Month Year]".

Rope Drop Plan: suggests an arrival time before park open based on today's predicted crowd level. Tip Board: "Worth it today?" names the single best return-pass pick right now.

My Day: the "take a break" nudge fires after 3 rides instead of 4 on a hot day. Live "walked ~X mi today" line. Visit History: "This Trip So Far" adds "Best day so far" and "Quietest trip yet".

Morning briefing notification (weather + crowd + top Must-Do pick), once per trip day 6–10am, when a Trip Planner trip covers today and this resort.

Must-Do alerts: "back up" notifications say how long it was down. Purple "One Last Ride?" capsule ~30 min before close.

Wait Times: "Down Right Now" and "Nearby & Short" strips; wait tile trend arrows; "closing soon" + "Quietest/busiest" line; rest-stop button; sensory chips; Easy Wins strip; "+15/-10 vs usual".

Smart Planner & My Day: flag free gaps ≥20 min with a nearby short-wait suggestion; "On this day" card; Best Park Today; plan a future day; Change the Plan shows standby time + walking; rain/heat routes indoor rides into bad weather.

Trip Planner: "Vacation Calendar" with a "Blocked Out" flag; Trip Budget pacing. Spending: "This Year" total; "Quick Snack" chips. My Dining: Mobile Order countdown. Ride Counter: "Your #1 ride". Recaps: "Year in Review". Visit History: past weather per day. Day Recap: "X per ride today". Ride page: Wait Forecast "you usually wait ~X".

Single Rider badge + filter (Universal). Add to My Day: one screen — rides/a show, then Anytime/Now/Best time/showtime/set time.
