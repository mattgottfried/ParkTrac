NEW: Japan ride heights + Super Nintendo World timed entry

Ride heights in cm (Tokyo Disney Resort & Universal Studios Japan)
• Ride cards and ride details at the Japan resorts show height minimums in cm (e.g. "102 cm"); Orlando stays in inches.
• The Flying Dinosaur and Raging Spirits also show their maximum height.
• These heights come from the parks' published requirements — please check them against the signs at each ride entrance in November.

Height Checker (Settings)
• New resort picker: Orlando / Tokyo Disney / USJ.
• Uses cm automatically in Japan (the "Use centimetres" switch still works, and your choice is remembered).
• Your child's height is remembered between visits.

Super Nintendo World timed entry (USJ)
• My Day at Universal Studios Japan has "Log Area Timed Entry". Enter the time from your USJ app ticket.
• It shows up with your Express Passes, starts a Live Activity, notifies you when the entry window opens and 10 minutes before it closes.

Please check:
• Switch to Tokyo Disney → Wait Times: height tags read in cm on rides like Big Thunder Mountain, Tower of Terror, Indiana Jones.
• Settings → Height Checker: switch resorts, flip cm/inches, step the height — the ride lists update.
• Switch to USJ → My Day → Log Area Timed Entry with a time a couple of minutes ahead — you get the "entry is open" notification.
