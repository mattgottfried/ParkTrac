Fixed since the last build

Smart Planner no longer adds a dinner you didn't pick
- Apple Intelligence was sometimes inventing a "Dinner at Be Our Guest" (even twice), which pushed your rides past park close.
- Now it only adds a meal or break when your notes ask for one (e.g. "dinner at 6:30"), and never the same one twice.
- Check: pick a few rides with no notes → Plan with Apple Intelligence → only your rides and chosen shows appear, no extra dining.
- Check: add "snack break at 3" in the notes → one snack break at 3.

Redesigned ride cards (Wait Times list)
- The wait now sits in a colored tile on the left of each card, so you can scan waits straight down the list. Down rides show an orange ⚠ tile, closed rides a red ✕.
- Must-Do rides show a yellow star next to the name.
- One line under the name for a Good Time deal (the card also gets a green outline) or why a ride isn't running.
- Lightning Lane, walk time, thrill dot and height sit together on one tidy line. The word "Operating" is gone.
- Check: the cards read cleanly, long names wrap, and tapping or long-pressing a card still works.

Easier to expand and shrink the ride list
- Drag or tap anywhere on the top of the panel (the park name and "Updated…" line), not just the little gray bar.
- The panel follows your finger while you drag, and a quick flick up or down is enough.
- Check: drag up to expand, drag down to shrink, tap the park name to toggle, and the Must-Do / filter buttons still work.

Redesigned ride page (tap any ride)
- Same colored wait tile as the list at the top, next to the name, park, status and how many times you've ridden it. The Must-Do star sits top-right.
- Four big buttons right under it: Rode It!, My Day, Alert (shows your alert, e.g. "≤30 min", once set) and Return.
- Ride info chips, then Lightning Lane / DAS-AAP, alerts, the wait stopwatch and predictions each sit in their own white card. The Dismiss button is gone (swipe down).
- Check: each button opens the right sheet, the star toggles, and long pages scroll smoothly.

My location button on the map
- New location-arrow button top-right on Wait Times: centers the map on you (street level), and the park filter follows.
- If location isn't allowed yet it asks; if you turned it off it offers to open Settings.

New Wait Forecast on the ride page (replaces "Predictions")
- A clear call at the top: "Good time to go now", or "Shorter around 8pm — saves about 25 min".
- Color-coded bars for each hour the park is open today. "Now" is labeled, past hours fade, and a green ★ marks the best hour left.
- Touch or drag across the chart to see any hour's expected wait.
- Lines underneath: whether it's getting longer or shorter next hour, and how today compares with the usual for this time.
- It uses the same per-ride numbers as the Smart Planner and Tip Board, so they all agree.
- Check: open a few rides at different times of day. The call and the chart should make sense, and the bars should stop at park close.

Community wait history (better forecasts for every ride)
- The ThrillTrack server now records every ride's wait every 10 minutes at all four resorts, all day, even when nobody has the app open.
- Once it has a couple of days, each ride's Wait Forecast, the Smart Planner and the Tip Board use that ride's real typical waits for today's day of the week. Your own visits still count first.
- The forecast says "Typical Friday" (etc.) in the corner when it's using this history.
- Check: this builds up over the first days, and a ride's forecast should shift from "Estimated" to "Typical <day>" after a couple of days of the same weekday or two days of any data.

Good Time to Ride works for every ride
- Good Time badges, the green strip and Must-Do alerts used to need your own history for a ride. Now they also use everyone's recorded waits, so they work on your first visit.

"You usually wait ~45 (posted 60)"
- Once you've timed a ride twice with the stopwatch (or any 5 rides at the resort), its ride page shows how long you really wait versus the posted time.

Rain plan
- ThrillTrack checks the hourly rain chance. When rain is likely, the Smart Planner and Next Up put indoor rides (dark rides, simulators, shows, Space Mountain…) in those hours.
- A blue "Rain likely 3–5 PM" note shows on Next Up and the planner, and a heads-up notification comes about half an hour before (Settings → Rain Heads-Up).

New Add to My Day
- Rides and shows are right on the screen. No more park → ride drill-down. Tap several rides to add them all at once.
- Pick when: Anytime, Now, the ride's Best time today, a showtime, or a set time.
- From a ride's page it opens small with that ride already picked.
- Lightning Lane returns logged here now also start the Live Activity and reminder.

No more pull-to-refresh on the ride list
- Pulling down on the list sometimes cancelled the refresh and showed "Couldn't refresh wait times" over rides that were fine. Pull-to-refresh is gone; waits still update on their own every minute.
- If waits are more than 5 minutes old, the orange "Wait times from N min ago" pill in the panel is now a button: tap it to refresh.
- A failed refresh no longer hides the rides you already have.

Tap a show
- Shows in the Wait Times list now open a show page: today's showtimes (the next one highlighted), a 🔔 on each future showing for a reminder 15 minutes before, and Add to My Day (opens with the show and its next showtime picked).

Redesigned Live Activities + new "Next Up" on the Lock Screen
- New look for every Live Activity: a colored icon for each type, a bigger countdown, and cleaner Dynamic Island views.
- Lightning Lane / Express returns show a progress bar across the return window ("Window open · closes in 42:10", 2:15 – 3:15 PM).
- The wait stopwatch fills a bar toward the posted wait, so you can see if the line is beating the sign.
- NEW: while a Smart Planner plan is running, a "Next Up" Live Activity shows your next ride with its wait tile, when to get in line, the walk, what's after it, "4 of 9 done" and any rain heads-up. It updates whenever the app refreshes wait times and ends when you end the plan. Turn it off in Settings → Next Up on Lock Screen.
- Check: start a plan → lock the phone → Next Up is there; long-press the Dynamic Island; log a Lightning Lane return and watch the bar.

Must-Do down alerts
- When one of your Must-Do (⭐) rides goes down you get "⚠️ … is down", and "✅ … is back up" with the posted wait when it's running again. It works all day, every time.
- With Instant Alerts on, these come from the alert server within about a minute even when ThrillTrack is closed; otherwise they arrive when the app refreshes.
- Settings → Must-Do Down Alerts turns them off.

Real crowd calendar
- The Crowd Calendar now uses ThrillTrack's recorded waits. Past days show the measured average wait (tap a day: "Measured: average wait ~38 min" with each park's number).
- The next two weeks are predicted from the same weekday over recent weeks ("Predicted from the last 3 Saturdays"). Further out it falls back to the seasonal estimate.
- This fills in as the server collects data. Check after a few days that past days show "Measured".

Day recap
- My Day → ✨ button (top right): today's recap card with rides, time in line, how much you beat the posted waits, your best-timed ride, repeat rides, steps and distance walked, and money spent.
- Share it as an image card or as text. Past days: Stats → Recaps.
- ThrillTrack asks for Motion & Fitness permission for the step count (the phone keeps about a week).

Live Activities, round two
- Resort colors: Disney is navy/blue with gold, Universal charcoal/teal with lime, Tokyo Disney navy/rose, USJ black/red.
- Big numbers: countdowns and the stopwatch are large and easy to read at a glance, with one line under them.
- Next Up is now a timeline of your next three stops (time, name, wait), with the next one highlighted.
- Buttons, no need to open the app: Next Up has Done / Skip, the stopwatch has "I'm On — Log It" (logs the ride with your real wait), and returns have "Used It" (ticks it off in My Day and ends the countdown).
- Check: tap each button on the Lock Screen and in the expanded Dynamic Island and confirm My Day / Ride Counter update.

Plan a future day
- Smart Planner has a Day picker (up to 60 days ahead). Pick rides and shows, and it plans that day using that day's park hours and typical waits for that weekday (your visits + everyone's recorded waits). Show times are copied from today's schedule.
- "Save Plan for Sat, Oct 10" keeps it in My Day → Coming Up. On the morning of that day it becomes your live Next Up plan automatically and re-plans from live waits.
- "Add to My Day as a Fixed List" puts the timed list on that day instead. Coming Up shows each future day; tap one to see, add or delete items. Add to My Day there skips Now/Best/Lightning Lane.
- My Day → Coming Up → Plan a Future Day opens the planner on tomorrow.
- Check: plan tomorrow, save it, see it under Coming Up; the next morning it should appear as Next Up.
