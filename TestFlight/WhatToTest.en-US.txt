New since the last build (short version: TestFlight allows 4,000 characters)

Smart Planner
- No more invented dinners: meals/breaks only when your notes ask ("snack break at 3").
- Plan a future day (Day picker, 60 days out) using that day's hours and typical waits. Save Plan → My Day → Coming Up; the morning of, it becomes your live Next Up plan.
- Change the Plan: shows total walking; one-tap Less Walking / Must-Dos First / Shortest Waits; with Apple Intelligence, type requests ("keep rides close together").
- Rain plan: indoor rides move into likely-rain hours, with a heads-up note and a notification ~30 min before.

Wait Times
- New ride cards: colored wait tile, Must-Do star, one info line. Drag/tap anywhere on the panel header to expand/shrink.
- My-location button on the map. Pull-to-refresh is gone (caused false errors); tap the "N min ago" pill instead.
- Shows open a show page: showtimes, 15-min reminders, Add to My Day.

Ride page
- New layout: wait tile header, 4 buttons (Rode It!, My Day, Alert, Return), cards.
- Wait Forecast replaces Predictions: a clear call ("Go now" / "Shorter at 8pm"), hourly bars, best hour ★.
- "You usually wait ~45 (posted 60)" after 2 timed rides. Forecasts/Good Time/Tip Board now use everyone's recorded waits, so they work day one.

Add to My Day: one screen — pick several rides/a show, then Anytime/Now/Best time/showtime/set time. LL returns start the Live Activity.

Live Activities: resort colors, big countdowns, progress bars. Next Up timeline (3 stops) while a plan runs. Buttons: Done/Skip, "I'm On — Log It", "Used It".

Alerts & stats
- Must-Do down alerts ("is down"/"is back up"). Crowd Calendar uses measured waits + predicts 2 weeks out.
- Day recap (My Day → ✨): rides, time in line, steps, spending; share as image/text. Past days: Stats → Recaps (asks for Motion & Fitness).

Car locator
- Sections/rows match the Disney/Universal apps; rows are a scroll wheel. Opening it guesses your section (from the lot map) and row (GPS + mapped rows) automatically.
- Lot maps: Magic Kingdom (12), Animal Kingdom (6), EPCOT (8), Hollywood Studios (5/6), Epic Universe (Explorer/Monster/Viking/Dragon/Hero — new, rows 501–511).
- Unmapped lots learn from saved spots (yours + everyone's, "Help Map This Lot"). CityWalk garages aren't guessed.
- Check: at a lot, section/row should fill in ("Guessed from…"); spin if off, save. Tell me if a Magic Kingdom/Animal Kingdom/Epic section comes up wrong.

Fixed: the upload was rejected — Siri's "Plan my day" description mentioned Apple (App Store rule 90626).

Ride photos: Rode It! (the full log sheet) has an optional "Add a Photo" step; Visit History shows a thumbnail — tap for full screen.

Trip budget: Spending → "Set a Trip Budget" — a progress bar (green/orange/red) tracks spend against it, with amount left or over. Pass Savings (top-left) is unchanged.

Offline, no more misleading wait times: with no signal, rides show "No signal" instead of a fake wait or a false "Closed", with a gray "Offline" banner. Check: Airplane Mode at a park, open Wait Times.

Lightning Lane cost auto-log: booking a paid Single Pass now has its own "I Booked It" button — logs the return AND the price into Spending automatically. Check: book a paid LL return, confirm it shows up in Spending under "Lightning Lane".

Heat heads-up: same idea as rain — the hottest hours of the day push indoor rides into that window in the Smart Planner/Next Up, with a heads-up note and a notification ~30 min before (Settings → Heat Heads-Up).

Character meet tracker: Bucket List has a new "Characters" tab — a checklist (met, date, notes, photo) seeded with 22 Orlando characters. Check: mark one met with a photo, confirm it shows in the list.
