NEW since the last build (car locator roll-up):

Time Sensitive alerts

Ride alerts now get through Focus modes (Sleep, Work, Driving, Do Not Disturb) and stay on the Lock Screen for an hour:
• Wait-time drop alerts (including DAS/AAP), Lightning Lane / Priority Pass openings, "back up" alerts
• Return time is open / closing soon, Super Nintendo World entry open
• "Parks close soon — your car is at…"
Confirmations ("… logged") and pass-renewal reminders stay normal.

The first time one arrives during a Focus, iOS asks whether to keep allowing ThrillTrack's Time Sensitive notifications — choose Allow. You can change it any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → ThrillTrack → Time Sensitive Notifications.

Please check:
• Turn on a Focus (e.g. Do Not Disturb), set a wait alert just above a ride's current wait, and lock the phone — the alert still comes through, labeled Time Sensitive.
• Instant Alerts (server pushes) come through the same way.
• Car locator, lot menus and Home Screen shortcuts from the last build still work.
