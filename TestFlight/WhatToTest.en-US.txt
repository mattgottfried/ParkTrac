NEW: Car Locator (with Disney-style lot menus) + Home Screen shortcuts
This build rolls up everything since the Instant Alerts build.

Save where you parked
• Tap the car button on the Wait Times map (top right), "Save Parking Spot" in My Day → Parking, or long-press the ThrillTrack icon → Find My Car.
• Standing by the car, tap "Save Spot Here" — ThrillTrack takes a precise GPS fix (a few seconds).
• Pick where you parked from menus, like the Disney app: Lot (Magic Kingdom, EPCOT, Hollywood Studios, Animal Kingdom, Disney Springs, CityWalk Garages, Epic Universe) → Section (e.g. Heroes/Villains → Zurg) → Row. Disney Springs garages also have a Level.
• Snap a photo of the row sign, and add a note if you like.
• No GPS needed: "Save Section & Row Only" saves just what you picked.

Find your car
• A blue car pin shows on the Wait Times map; the car button turns blue.
• Open it for a mini map, distance and walk time, and "Walk There in Maps" for walking directions.
• The spot and note sync to your other devices on the same iCloud account; the photo stays on the phone that took it.
• A spot lasts the park day (20 hours). "Move Spot to Here" and "Clear Spot" are in the sheet.
• No GPS (location off, deep garage)? The note and photo still save.

Reminder before parks close
• With a spot saved, you get "Parks close at 9:00 PM — Your car: Magic Kingdom · Zurg · Row 112" 30 minutes before the last park closes (after-hours parties don't count). Tap it for directions. Turn it off in the parking sheet.

Home Screen shortcuts
• Long-press the ThrillTrack icon: Find My Car, My Day, Wait Times.
