NEW since the last build (car locator roll-up):

Good Time to Ride
• Rides whose wait is well below usual get a green "Good time · usually ~70" tag, and a "Good Time to Ride" strip sits at the top of the ride list (Must-Dos first).
• "Usual" comes from the waits ThrillTrack has recorded on this phone — the same time of day on earlier days, or earlier today. It needs a few visits' worth of data, so it gets better the more you use the app.
• Must-Do rides send one heads-up per ride per day ("Slinky Dog Dash 35 min now — usually ~70 min at this time"). Turn it off in Settings → Good Time to Ride Alerts.

Trip Planner (Stats → Trip Planner)
• "Plan Japan Trip" sets up a trip with a countdown (also shown at the top of My Day), and a pre-trip checklist: passports, Visit Japan Web, Tokyo Disney / USJ tickets and apps, Suica, eSIM, yen and more. Edit, reorder, add or delete items; it syncs to Heather's phone.
• Yen ↔ dollar: live rate (or set your own). At the Japan resorts, Spending shows ≈ $ beside every yen amount, and Add Purchase takes yen.

Time Sensitive alerts
• Ride alerts get through Focus modes (Sleep, Work, Driving). The first time, iOS asks — choose Allow.
