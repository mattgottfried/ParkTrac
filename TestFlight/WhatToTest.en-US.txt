New since the last build (short version: TestFlight allows 4,000 characters)

Smart Planner
- No more invented dinners: meals/breaks only when your notes ask ("snack break at 3").
- Plan a future day: Day picker (60 days ahead) uses that day's hours and typical waits. Save Plan → My Day → Coming Up; that morning it becomes your live Next Up plan.
- Change the Plan: shows total walking; one tap for Less Walking, Must-Dos First, Shortest Waits; with Apple Intelligence type requests ("keep rides close together").
- Rain plan: when rain is likely, indoor rides go in the wet hours, a "Rain likely 3–5 PM" note shows, and a heads-up comes ~30 min before.

Wait Times
- New ride cards: colored wait tile on the left, Must-Do star, one tidy info line.
- Drag or tap anywhere on the panel header to expand/shrink the list.
- My-location button top-right on the map.
- Pull-to-refresh is gone (it caused false errors); tap the orange "N min ago" pill to refresh.
- Shows open a show page: showtimes, 15-min reminders, Add to My Day.

Ride page
- New layout: wait tile header, four buttons (Rode It!, My Day, Alert, Return), cards.
- Wait Forecast replaces Predictions: "Good time to go now" or "Shorter around 8pm", hourly bars (touch to read), best hour ★.
- "You usually wait ~45 (posted 60)" after you've timed a ride twice.
- Forecasts, Good Time badges and the Tip Board now use everyone's recorded waits (server history), so they work on day one.

Add to My Day
- One screen: pick several rides or a show, then Anytime / Now / Best time / showtime / set time. Lightning Lane returns start the Live Activity.

Live Activities
- Resort colors, big countdowns, progress bars for return windows and the stopwatch.
- Next Up on the Lock Screen while a plan runs: timeline of the next 3 stops.
- Buttons: Done / Skip, "I'm On — Log It", "Used It". Settings → Next Up on Lock Screen.

Alerts and stats
- Must-Do down alerts: "is down" / "is back up" (Settings → Must-Do Down Alerts).
- Crowd Calendar uses measured waits for past days and predicts the next two weeks.
- Day recap (My Day → ✨): rides, time in line, steps, spending; share as image or text. Past days: Stats → Recaps. Asks for Motion & Fitness.

Car locator
- Sections and rows match the Disney/Universal apps; rows are a scroll wheel.
- It guesses your section and row from GPS when you open it. Lot maps now cover Magic Kingdom (all 12 sections), Animal Kingdom (Peacock, Butterfly, Unicorn, Dinosaur, Giraffe, Yeti), EPCOT (all 8), Hollywood Studios (5 of 6) and Epic Universe (Explorer, Monster, Viking, Dragon, Hero — Hero is new: rows 501–511).
- Unmapped lots learn from saved spots (yours, plus everyone's with "Help Map This Lot" on). CityWalk garages aren't guessed.
- Check: at a lot, open the car locator. The section and row should be filled in ("Guessed from the lot map"). Spin the wheel if the row is off, then save. Your first save in a section sets which way its rows count. Magic Kingdom, Animal Kingdom and Epic sections were drawn from the lot diagrams, so say if a section comes up wrong.
